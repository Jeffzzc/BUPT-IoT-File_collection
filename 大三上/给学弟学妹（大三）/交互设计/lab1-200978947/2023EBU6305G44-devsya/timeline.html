Impact

Economic Development     

Economic Development

Impact on economic development: the impact of the third scientific and technological revolution on economic development is shown in the following aspects; First, it has caused changes in various factors of productivity and significantly improved labor productivity; The third scientific and technological revolution not only strengthened the trend of non-material industrial structure and intelligent production process, but also caused changes in the economic layout of various countries and the world economic structure; In addition, the third scientific and technological revolution developed management into a real science with its rich content and realized modernization.

Social Life     

Impact on social life and people's Modernization: the third scientific and technological revolution not only brought about the modernization of things and caused changes in the way of labor and life, but also created a new generation to adapt to it and gradually modernize people's ideas, ways of thinking, ways of behavior and ways of life.

Social Life

Human Society     

Human Society

Impact on human society: the invention and wide use of electronic computers and the formation of various "man-machine control systems" in the third scientific and technological revolution made it possible to realize the automation of production, office and family life (the so-called "three a" revolution). It indicates that human society will enter another higher automation era from the era of mechanization and electrification; The development of space technology and marine technology indicates that human society has entered a far-reaching three-dimensional new era of land, sea and air from the era of "earth residents" bound to the earth's surface; The progress of gene recombination technology, structural chemistry and molecular engineering has enabled mankind to obtain the creativity of actively creating new organisms and new life, marking that mankind is moving step by step from the "Kingdom of necessity" to the "Kingdom of freedom".

Capitalism     

Impact on Capitalism: the third scientific and technological revolution promoted the transition of capitalism from general monopoly to state monopoly. Because the third scientific and technological revolution has greatly promoted the development of social productive forces and continuously improved the degree of socialization of production, the original private monopoly can not meet the needs of the rapid development of productive forces. Therefore, it is urgent for the full development of state monopoly and the comprehensive intervention of the state in the economy.

Capitalism

World Economy     

World Economy

Impact on the world economy: the third scientific and technological revolution raised the level of world productivity and accelerated the recovery and development of the world economy after the war; It has promoted the development of international trade, the change of world monetary and financial relations and the international flow of production factors; It has promoted the development of multinational corporations and international economic integration, and has caused changes in the world economic structure and economic strategy.

International Relations     

Impact on International Relations: the third scientific and technological revolution has had a profound impact on international relations. On the one hand, it intensifies the imbalance in the development of capitalist countries and makes new changes in the international status of capitalist countries; On the other hand, it gives socialist countries a strong driving force in the struggle against western capitalist countries. At the same time, the third scientific and technological revolution widened the gap between the rich and the poor around the world and promoted the changes of social production relations around the world.

International Relations

Global Issues     

Global Issues

Impact on global issues: on the one hand, the development of scientific and technological revolution has expanded the field of human activities to transform nature and improved human ability to fight against nature, so as to promote the material and spiritual civilization of human society to a new height unimaginable by predecessors; On the other hand, it also brings a series of thorny social problems, such as the deterioration of the ecological environment, the excessive consumption of natural resources and energy and the threat of nuclear disaster. The uncontrollable malignant development of these problems has increasingly troubled the human situation and become a global problem of global concern.

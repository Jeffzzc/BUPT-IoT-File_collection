Shapowei Zone - Experience the Real Xiamen

    Xiamen is a city on the rise. What has been a popular tourist attraction for locals is now becoming popular among the expat crowd. If you are thinking of coming to Xiamen, there will be many exciting things to do, foods to eat, and sights to see. However, if you want to enjoy some of the genuine local culture, there is no place better than Shapowei.

    At the heart of Shapowei's local culture is the Shapowei Art Zone. Every weekend, local artists come here to sell their wares. One can also grab a slice of pizza and a cold beverage while admiring the local street art and checking out the skaters performing tricks at the skate park.

    Address: Shapowei Art Zone, 60 Daxue Road (厦门沙坡尾艺术西区60号之13设备房).

    Down the road from the art zone, hidden away from the crowds, is Thank You Cafe. Thank You Cafe is a combination coffee house and specialty drink bar with a jazz club feel. Coming to this cafe, one can enjoy the best handmade drink in Xiamen. No mojitos from a shaker here. On the second floor, light dishes are made fresh and served daily, and at night you will hear eclectic music ranging from Jazz to hip hop coming from the turntables. In keeping with the feel of the cafe, there is no sign or obvious entrance outside so make sure to give the driver the address.

    Address: Shop 8-9, 162 Daxue Road, Siming District, Xiamen, Fujian (厦门市思明区大学路162号8-9店面).

    If you are feeling peckish and desire something light and sweet to eat, there is only one place to go: Duoji Doughnuts. Owned by a local artist, Douji offers an array of unique flavors and aesthetically pleasing treats. Stop in and see why these small sweet treats are a favorite among locals.

    Address: 113 Daxue Road, Siming District, Xiamen (厦门市思明区大学路113号).

    Best Time: March ~ May and September ~ October

    Recommended Visiting Hours: 1 ~ 2 days

    Open Hours: All day

    Tickets: The zone is free, however, some of the attractions on the zone have entrance ticket. See detailed information below

    Address: Shapowei Art Zone, 60 Daxue Road, Xiamen 361002, China

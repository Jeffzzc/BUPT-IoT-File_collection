Seven Xiamen businesses appear at 3rd CICPE

    The third China International Consumer Products Expo, also known as the CICPE, is taking place in Haikou, South China's Hainan province, from April 10 to 15. This year, Xiamen has built a 300-square-meter theme hall at the exhibition site, while seven high-quality independent companies from the city will appear at the expo.

    The main color of the Xiamen Pavillion is blue, which is closely associated with coastal cities. With a theme of “digital wave and boutique Xiamen,” the Xiamen Pavilion is focusing on sports intelligence and fashion life to provide immersive product experiences for consumers.

    The seven local companies appearing at the Xiamen Pavilion are Ogawa, HPRT Electronic Technology, Golden Huanan Group, City Spark, Sunnypet, Wild Land, and Xiamen Tea.

    For the expo, Wild Land will bring the world's first wireless remote controlled car roof tent to showcase a new camping lifestyle. City Spark will highlight the GMT for Kids children's spine protection backpack and it had received many inquiries from venue exhibitors during the exhibition period..

    Ogawa is bringing its new product AI massage robot 2.0 that supports Huawei's HarmonyOS Connect, while the latest AR photo printer from HPRT Electronic Technology will make its debut at the expo.

    The exhibition area of the expo has increased by 20 percent compared to the last edition, gathering over 3,100 renowned brands from over 60 countries and regions. During the exhibition, more than 100 activities, including forum discussions, results releases, trade negotiations, investment promotion, and other activities, will also be held.
